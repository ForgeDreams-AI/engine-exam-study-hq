## Air Brake Test (Q1–Q10)

**Q1.** What is Step 1 of the air brake test?
A) Engine off, power and ignition on
B) Set wheel chocks properly
C) Push in the parking brake
D) Test the service brake
**Answer: B** — *Chocks go on before any brake test begins (Step 1).*

**Q2.** With the parking brake released (pushed in), the maximum allowable air drop after 1 minute is:
A) 1 PSI
B) 2 PSI
C) 3 PSI
D) 5 PSI
**Answer: B** — *Parking-brake-released leakage test: no more than 2 PSI in 1 minute.*

**Q3.** With the foot (service) brake applied, the maximum allowable initial drop in 1 minute is:
A) 2 PSI
B) 3 PSI
C) 4 PSI
D) 6 PSI
**Answer: B** — *Service-brake leakage test: no more than 3 PSI in 1 minute — don't confuse with the 2 PSI parking-brake test.*

**Q4.** When pumping the brake to test the low pressure warning signal, it should activate at:
A) 40 PSI
B) 60 PSI
C) 80 PSI
D) 100 PSI
**Answer: B** — *Low pressure warning: falls to 60 PSI.*

**Q5.** Spring brakes activate between:
A) 10–20 PSI
B) 20–40 PSI
C) 40–60 PSI
D) 60–80 PSI
**Answer: B** — *Spring brakes: 20–40 PSI.*

**Q6.** The air pressure build-up rate check is performed at:
A) Idle
B) High idle or 1500 RPM
C) 2000 RPM
D) Any RPM
**Answer: B** — *Build-up check: high idle or 1500 RPM.*

**Q7.** At high idle/1500 RPM, pressure must build from 85 to 100 PSI within:
A) 30 seconds
B) 45 seconds
C) 60 seconds
D) 90 seconds
**Answer: B** — *85 to 100 PSI within 45 seconds.*

**Q8.** To check compressor CUT-IN, you pump the brake down to:
A) 60 PSI
B) 85 PSI
C) 100 PSI
D) 120 PSI
**Answer: C** — *Cut-in: pump down to 100 PSI and watch for air build-up. Cut-OUT is 120–125.*

**Q9.** Compressor cut-out occurs between:
A) 100–110 PSI
B) 110–120 PSI
C) 120–125 PSI
D) 125–135 PSI
**Answer: C** — *Cut-out: 120–125 PSI — the same range the system builds to in Step 7.*

**Q10.** Steps 10–12 in order are:
A) Test parking brake, test service brake, remove chocks
B) Remove chocks, test service brake, test parking brake
C) Remove wheel chocks, test parking brake, test service brake
D) Test service brake, remove chocks, test parking brake
**Answer: C** — *Chocks come off (10) before the final parking-brake (11) and service-brake (12) tests.*

## Code 3 Emergency Response (Q11–Q18)

**Q11.** Maximum speed over the posted limit under Code 3:
A) 5 mph
B) 10 mph
C) 15 mph
D) 20 mph
**Answer: B** — *Rule 1: maximum 10 mph over posted.*

**Q12.** In center or oncoming traffic lanes, after the brief deceleration period, maximum speed is:
A) 10 mph
B) 15 mph
C) 20 mph
D) 25 mph
**Answer: C** — *Rule 2: max 20 mph in center/oncoming lanes.*

**Q13.** Code 3 in the oncoming lane approaching a GREEN traffic light — you:
A) Proceed through at 20 mph
B) Slow but continue through
C) Complete stop regardless of light color
D) Stop only if cross traffic is present
**Answer: C** — *Rule 3: complete stop at ALL stop signs and traffic lights regardless of color in center/oncoming lanes.*

**Q14.** Traveling WITH traffic under Code 3, you must make a complete stop at:
A) All intersections
B) All red lights and stop signs
C) Only stop signs
D) Only red lights
**Answer: B** — *Rule 4: complete stop at all red lights and stop signs when with traffic.*

**Q15.** In an ACTIVE school zone under Code 3:
A) Exceed the limit by 10 mph as usual
B) Lights and sirens may be used but do NOT exceed the posted speed limit
C) No lights or sirens permitted
D) Drop to Code 1
**Answer: B** — *Rule 5: lights/sirens OK, never exceed posted limit in active school zones.*

**Q16.** In an active school zone, what should you consider when conditions warrant?
A) Code 1
B) Code 2
C) Code 4
D) Full stop
**Answer: B** — *Rule 5: consider Code 2 when conditions warrant.*

**Q17.** Which is TRUE about the "brief deceleration period"?
A) It applies on all roads at all times
B) It applies before the 20 mph max in center/oncoming lanes
C) It replaces the need to stop at red lights
D) It only applies in school zones
**Answer: B** — *Rule 2 ties the deceleration period to the 20 mph center/oncoming-lane max.*

**Q18.** The key difference between Rule 3 and Rule 4 is:
A) Speed limits
B) Whether you are in center/oncoming lanes vs traveling with traffic
C) Time of day
D) Vehicle type
**Answer: B** — *Rule 3 (center/oncoming): stop at everything regardless of color. Rule 4 (with traffic): stop at red lights and stop signs.*

## Phase 2 Pumping Exercise (Q19–Q32)

**Q19.** In Phase 2, the key pumper position is taken by:
A) The first engineer of drivers training
B) The second engineer
C) The candidate
D) The practor
**Answer: C** — *Candidate = key pumper; first engineer = forward pumper; second engineer handjacks supply hose.*

**Q20.** Time starts when:
A) You get in the truck
B) The truck is put in pump
C) Your foot hits the ground after exiting with vest and radio
D) The first hose is deployed
**Answer: C** — *Exit with safety vest and radio — time starts when foot hits ground.*

**Q21.** The first priority once time starts is:
A) Placing wheel chocks
B) Pumping the humat
C) Opening the intake
D) Radioing the practor
**Answer: B** — *First priority: properly pump the humat.*

**Q22.** The correct humat hose sequence is:
A) Two lengths coupled together, then to intake, then to humat
B) First length to front of humat → double male on hose → second length via double male → second double male to intake, then to second hose
C) Double male to intake first, then both hose lengths to humat
D) Single length to humat, double female to intake
**Answer: B** — *Exact sequence: hose → front of humat, double male, second hose via double male, second double male to intake then to second hose.*

**Q23.** The rear supply line connects:
A) Male end to discharge, female end to humat
B) Female end to truck discharge (passenger N.1), male end to side of humat
C) Both ends to the humat
D) Female end to intake, male end to discharge
**Answer: B** — *Female to truck discharge (passenger N.1); male to side of humat.*

**Q24.** The correct valve-opening sequence is:
A) Intake → butterfly → discharge
B) Butterfly → discharge → intake
C) Discharge to humat FIRST → butterfly valve → intake (keystone)
D) All three simultaneously
**Answer: C** — *Discharge to humat first, flip butterfly, then intake (keystone) — order is critical.*

**Q25.** Until the forward pumper calls for more water, you pump at:
A) Maximum 150 psi immediately
B) Equal pressure to your intake pressure
C) 100 psi
D) Idle pressure
**Answer: B** — *Equal to intake pressure until told otherwise; max 150 psi after.*

**Q26.** Maximum pump pressure in Phase 2:
A) 100 psi
B) 120 psi
C) 150 psi
D) 200 psi
**Answer: C** — *Maximum 150 psi.*

**Q27.** Every reverse operation requires:
A) A double male only
B) A double female only
C) A double male AND a double female
D) No special couplings
**Answer: C** — *Every reverse operation: double male AND double female.*

**Q28.** From the key pumper in the reverse operation, which coupling connects to the discharge?
A) Double male
B) Double female
C) Reducer
D) Butterfly valve
**Answer: B** — *From the key pumper, the double female connects to the discharge.*

**Q29.** Before sending water, the engineer must:
A) Open all discharges
B) Communicate face-to-face or over the radio that they are ready for water
C) Place wheel chocks
D) Shut down the key system
**Answer: B** — *Imperative: communicate readiness face-to-face or by radio before sending.*

**Q30.** Critical Factor #1:
A) Intake never below 20–30 psi
B) Intake never below 100 psi
C) Discharge never above 150 psi
D) Tank always full
**Answer: A** — *Never allow intake to drop below 20–30 psi.*

**Q31.** When operating the 2-stage pump, the truck must be in:
A) Drive
B) Reverse
C) Neutral — neutral going in / neutral going out
D) Park
**Answer: C** — *Critical Factor #5: NEUTRAL, neutral going in / neutral going out.*

**Q32.** The Phase 2 shutdown requires the pump at what pressure before idle?
A) 150 psi
B) 120 psi
C) 100 psi or less
D) 60 psi
**Answer: C** — *Bring pump to 100 psi or less before idle.*

## Engineer Practical Test Checklist (Q33–Q44)

**Q33.** The correct cab-prep order is:
A) Start truck → PTO → Drive → vest → radio
B) Seatbelt + vest on → start truck, verify Neutral → engage Dual Stage PTO → shift to Drive, verify two green lights → remove seatbelt, grab radio, look both ways
C) Vest on → Drive → PTO → Neutral → radio
D) Start truck → Drive → two green lights → PTO → exit
**Answer: B** — *Exact 5-step cab sequence; PTO engages in Neutral BEFORE Drive.*

**Q34.** After shifting to Drive, you verify:
A) One green light
B) Two green lights
C) RPM drop
D) Intake pressure
**Answer: B** — *Two green lights confirm pump engagement.*

**Q35.** At the front tire you verbalize:
A) "Intake above 20 PSI"
B) "Wheel chocks will be set later"
C) "Tank-to-Pump is open"
D) "PAR complete"
**Answer: B** — *Wheel chocks come later — say it at the front tire.*

**Q36.** Before opening a discharge at idle, verify:
A) Keystone is open
B) Tank-to-Pump is OPEN
C) Ladder drains are open
D) Chocks are set
**Answer: B** — *Tank-to-Pump must be verified OPEN first.*

**Q37.** The two supply sections deploy:
A) Both to the Keystone
B) One to the top of the truck, one to the Keystone
C) Both to the top of the truck
D) One to the crosslay, one to the Keystone
**Answer: B** — *One section to the top of the truck, one to the Keystone.*

**Q38.** The hose clamp is opened:
A) Kicked open quickly
B) Facing the hazard — DO NOT kick it open
C) After the Keystone is closed
D) Only during shutdown
**Answer: B** — *Open facing the hazard; kicking it is a critical error.*

**Q39.** Opening the WRONG discharge results in:
A) A warning
B) A point deduction
C) An automatic FAIL
D) A re-test of that step only
**Answer: C** — *Wrong discharge = AUTOMATIC FAIL. Verify BEFORE opening.*

**Q40.** Before opening the ladder discharge, confirm:
A) Ladder drains are OPEN
B) Ladder drains are closed and the ladder is ready for water
C) The handline is shut down
D) Tank Fill is open
**Answer: B** — *Drains closed + ladder ready for water — verbalize it.*

**Q41.** If supply lines begin to separate, deploy:
A) Another crosslay
B) An additional 100 ft of supply hose with the clamp upstream of the failure
C) The 2nd water supply immediately
D) Wheel chocks
**Answer: B** — *Extra 100 ft of supply hose, clamp placed upstream of the failure.*

**Q42.** Tank Fill is opened for which two reasons?
A) Fill tank + raise pressure
B) Cool pump + mark gauges
C) Fill the tank + cool the pump
D) Drain tank + test intake
**Answer: C** — *Two reasons: fill the tank and cool the pump — feel the water to verify.*

**Q43.** The exact PTO disengagement sequence is:
A) PTO off → Neutral → shut down
B) Neutral → wait 5 sec → PTO first position → wait 5 sec → PTO final position
C) Drive → Neutral → PTO off
D) PTO final position → wait 5 sec → Neutral
**Answer: B** — *Neutral, 5 sec, PTO first, 5 sec, PTO final — exact order and pauses.*

**Q44.** The 360° walk-around happens:
A) Before opening Tank Fill
B) After feeling the water / cooling, before closing the Keystone
C) After shutting down the truck
D) Before cab prep
**Answer: B** — *Order: Tank Fill cooling → feel water → 360° walk-around → close Keystone → verify panel → chocks → PTO sequence.*
